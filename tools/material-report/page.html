How the monthly loop works

Each month
  • Cost is CJI3 goods issues (document type WA) only. SAP's subtotal rows are dropped and the detail is tied to the printed grand total.
  • Each cost line is matched to its MB51 movement through the reference document (material document + year + item), so quantity and cost always belong together.
  • MB51 movements are classified – received from vendors, from stores, owner supplied, issued to project, to subcontractors, to orders, scrap, transfers – and summed into a quantity chain and stock balance per material.
  • ME2N gives each material its group, the PO prices and what is still to be delivered. Material groups suggest the Package; code on the Coding tab.

Quantity chain and cost per material

Quantities are in each material's MB51 unit and signed as they move stock: in +, out −. Stock balance is the sum of the chain, so it is only as complete as the MB51 date range. Direct / asset receipts go straight to an order or asset and never enter stock. Click a row for its months, PO lines and movements.

PO price against issue price

PO price = net price ÷ price unit, purchase lines not deleted · issue price = CJI3 cost ÷ qty issued

Material coding

One code per material, used for every line of it. Dashed badges are suggestions from the material group (M03… → DIV 03, M0302… → DIV 0302, SM/SP/CS… → INDIRECT); they count only after you accept them.
